## Verdict (curator judgement)

Sage Morning and Bottle Green Ledger, Bare Skin and Clay and Linen, Dusty Blue Room and Cabin Navy differ only in figure lightness: one hue on almost the same near-white ground, once as a mid-tone and once as a deep tone. Six of the 7 grounds are within ΔEOK 0.03 of each other, so the group is really one idea, a near-white ground with one muted figure, varied by hue and figure depth. Kraft and Black is the exception, with a tan ground.

No pair that is close also shares a domain, so no query shows two of them together, and the build's rule that library directions differ on at least one measure holds. **Keep all seven** (Phase 2 D4). For future curation, a new Light + Muted direction would add little unless it changes the ground; the pause in `library/policy.json` already stops the group from growing.
